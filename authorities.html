Quality Assurance

Faculty Quality Assurance Cell (FQAC)

The Wayamba University of Sri Lanka is dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of teaching, research and scholarship, and the enhancement of society’s core values by ensuring students’ achievement in excellent learning outcomes and enhanced educational opportunities.

The Faculty of Business Studies & Finance of Wayamba University of Sri Lanka has established a Faculty Quality Assurance Cell (FQAC), which coordinates quality assurance activities for the improvements in overall performance of the faculty in liaison with the Centre for Quality Assurance (CQA) of the University.

Accordingly, the FQAC of the Faculty of Business Studies and Finance was established with a directive to create a culture that pursues to continually improve the quality of all academic and other activities in the Faculty.

The ultimate intention of FQAC is to strengthen the Faculty’s capacity and commitment to produce quality graduates in disciplines of Accountancy & Business Finance, Banking & Finance, Business Management and Insurance & Finance.

FQAC Structure

  • Committee of academic staff chaired by the Dean
  • Coordinated by an appointed Faculty Coordinator
  • Liaises with the Centre for Quality Assurance (CQA)
  • Coordinates the Quality Assurance Units of the four departments
  • Facilitates programme reviews of the four departments

What FQAC Does

  • Plans, implements and evaluates quality assurance activities
  • Gives direction on best practices among staff and students
  • Coordinates quality assurance aspects with the QA Units of the four academic departments
  • Facilitates programme reviews of the four academic departments

Liaison Committees

  • Curriculum and Academic Development Committee
  • Research and Higher Degree Committee
  • Teaching and Learning Committee
  • Student-Staff Liaison Committee
