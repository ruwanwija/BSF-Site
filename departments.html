Departments of the Faculty

Four academic departments deliver the faculty’s degree, postgraduate, diploma and external programmes. Each department maintains its own vision, industry partnerships and research culture.

Department of Accountancy

Pioneers of business education at Wayamba University

Pioneering role in business education since the establishment of the University in 2001. Offers the B.Sc in Accountancy and Business Finance degree across three academic clusters — Accountancy, Business Finance and Accounting Information Systems — accredited by ACCA, CIMA, CA Sri Lanka and CMA.

Discover the department

Department of Banking & Finance

Pioneer in banking and finance education in Sri Lanka

One of the pioneer academic departments in the faculty, offering the B.Sc Banking and Finance (Special) degree since 2001. Focus areas include Economics, Bank Management, Investments, and Financial Markets and Institutions, with outcome-based education and six-month industry internships.

Discover the department

Department of Business Management

Management education from diploma to masters

Offers the Postgraduate Diploma in Business Management (PGDBM) leading to the Masters Degree in Business Administration (MBA). The PGDBM comprises twelve compulsory course modules and a project report; the MBA year combines two taught courses with a comprehensive research thesis.

Discover the department

Department of Insurance & Valuation

Pioneer academic center in risk and insurance education

Committed to enhancing education in Insurance and Finance through undergraduate, diploma and higher diploma programmes. Successfully conducts internal and external programmes for insurance industry employees and plans a postgraduate degree in Risk Management and Insurance.
