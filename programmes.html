How to Apply

1. Check Requirements

Review the entry requirements for your chosen programme on the cards above and confirm eligibility before applying.

2. Obtain the Application Form

Application forms and brochures referenced in the faculty documentation can be requested through the faculty office.

3. Submit & Enquire

Submit the completed application through the University’s administrative office and contact the programme contact for confirmation.
