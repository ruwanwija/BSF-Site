### 2.1 Department of Accountancy (DoA)

**Tagline:** Pioneers of business education at Wayamba University

#### Introduction

The Department of Accountancy at Wayamba University of Sri Lanka has played a pioneering role in business education since the establishment of the University in 2001, being one of the earliest academic disciplines introduced. At present, the Department offers the B.Sc in Accountancy and Business Finance degree, designed to meet the evolving demands of the accounting and business environment. The degree programme is structured into three key academic clusters — Accountancy, Business Finance, and Accounting Information Systems — designed to provide students with a comprehensive and industry-relevant education.

#### Vision

> An Assured future for all Sri Lankans and a prosperous society through learning

#### Mission

> The department will ensure the provision of high-quality accountancy education and training towards optimum utilization, control and development of resources that; raise achievement reduces disparity leads to opportunities

#### Message from the Head of the Department

The Department is strongly committed to delivering high-quality accounting education with a holistic approach to developing competent and ethical business professionals. Our academic staff comprise well-qualified scholars with doctoral training, relevant industry experience, and professional credentials in accountancy and related fields. They actively engage in research across diverse areas, including financial and management accounting, auditing, financial management, accounting education, taxation, and corporate governance. Our teaching and learning practices emphasize strong theoretical foundations, professional competencies, and innovative pedagogical approaches. The accounting programmes offered by the Department are accredited by leading professional bodies such as the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA), the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA), the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (CA Sri Lanka), and the Institute of Certified Management Accountants (CMA). These accreditations enhance the professional recognition and career prospects of our graduates. Graduates of the Department are well prepared to serve in managerial and professional capacities across a wide range of organizations, including accounting and auditing firms, financial institutions, corporate entities, government bodies, and non-profit organizations, both in Sri Lanka and beyond.

#### Research

Academic staff actively engage in research across diverse areas, including financial and management accounting, auditing, financial management, accounting education, taxation, and corporate governance. The department publishes the Journal of Accountancy & Finance (JAF) and the open-access Journal of SACFIRE, and participates in the SACFIRE symposium and the faculty-level JARSYM journal.

### 2.2 Department of Banking and Finance (DoBF)

**Tagline:** Pioneer in banking and finance education in Sri Lanka

#### Introduction

The Department of Banking & Finance is one of the pioneer academic departments in the Faculty of Business Studies and Finance, which offers the B.Sc Banking and Finance (Special) degree programme every year to the undergraduates of the faculty since 2001. The Department of Banking and Finance is committed to providing a well-rounded banking and finance education and co-curricular experience to all its students. The department is basically focused on the subject areas of Economics, Bank Management, Investments, Financial Markets and Institutions. The primary focus of the department is to enhance undergraduate and postgraduate learning and knowledge by teaching particularly in Banking & Finance and technology, and in close association with industry and commerce.

#### Vision

> To be the center of excellence in banking and finance education and research in Sri Lanka.

#### Mission

> To achieve excellence in the diffusion of knowledge relate to Banking and Financial services in line with the best practices for the field of Banking and Finance

#### Message from the Head of the Department

**Mr. AGDLK Gunaratna**

The Department of Banking & Finance is the pioneer department to offer a specialized degree in Banking & Finance in Sri Lanka. ### 3.2 Postgraduate Programmes

#### Postgraduate Diploma in Business Management (PGDBM)

**ID:** `pgdbm` · **Department:** Department of Business Management

**Summary:** One-year postgraduate diploma with twelve taught modules and a project report, leading to the MBA.

#### Programme Description

The Postgraduate Diploma in Business Management (PGDBM) is a programme of study which will run through one academic year (approximately one calendar year duration). Participants will follow twelve course modules during the period, of which all course modules will be compulsory. Each course module will be of about 30 hours of teaching. The academic year comprises two terms; twelve courses are taught during this period. In addition, every participant should complete a project report and submit the same at the end of the 2nd term of the Programme. Those who complete all the requirements of the PGDBM may be admitted to the 2nd year of the Programme — the Masters Degree in Business Administration.

#### Description

A publication of Students' Research of the Annual Research Symposium in Management. The Journal of ARSYM (JARSYM) is a refereed journal published bi-annually by the Faculty of Business Studies & Finance, Wayamba University of Sri Lanka. The aim of the JARSYM is to disseminate high-quality research findings on a variety of timely topics generated by undergraduate researchers at the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka. Furthermore, it opens up avenues for undergraduates involved in the industry to share their inventions, state-of-the-art discoveries, and novel ideas. The main philosophy behind the JARSYM is to enhance the research culture within the faculty, thereby within Wayamba University. All research articles submitted are double-blind reviewed prior to publishing. Views expressed in the research articles are not the views of the Faculty of Business Studies and Finance, Wayamba University of Sri Lanka, or the Editorial Board.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

**8 FAQs** are documented.

### Q1. Where is the Faculty of Business Studies and Finance located?

The faculty is located at the Kuliyapitiya premises of Wayamba University of Sri Lanka (60200, Kuliyapitiya, Sri Lanka).

### Q2. When was the faculty established?

The Faculty of Business Studies and Finance (FBSF) was established on 01 October 1999 by the Government Notification in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 1093/8 of Tuesday, 17 August 1999, and remains the fastest growing faculty of Wayamba University of Sri Lanka.

### Q3. How many departments does the faculty have?

The faculty consists of four departments of studies: Department of Accountancy, Department of Banking and Finance, Department of Business Management, and Department of Insurance and Valuation.

### Q4. What are the admission requirements for the Diploma in Accounting Information Systems (DAIS)?

Any one of the following: passed G.C.E Advanced Level in any stream; passed AAT second Level; passed ICASL executive Level; passed CIMA 1st Level; passed CMA 1st Level; passed ACCA 1st Level. Other qualifications may be acceptable case by case subject to approval of the Board of Study. Lectures are held on weekends.

### Q5. What are the admission routes for the Diploma in Banking and Financial Studies (DBFS)?

(1) Passed G.C.E (A/L) examination in any stream in a single attempt; (2) Six passes in G.C.E (O/L) Examination in one sitting with three credits and two years working experience in a reputed institution; (3) Any other qualification with two years working experience, considered case by case by the Board of Study and the Senate of WUSL. A good knowledge of English and performance at the admission test/interview are required.

### Q6. How does the PGDBM lead to the MBA?

The Postgraduate Diploma in Business Management (PGDBM) runs for one academic year with twelve compulsory course modules (24 credits) plus a project report. Those who complete all PGDBM requirements may be admitted to the second year — the Masters Degree in Business Administration (MBA) — earning 16 credits through two taught courses (8 credits) and a comprehensive research thesis (8 credits). A limited number of direct-entrance candidates may also be admitted to the MBA.

### Q7. How do I submit a research paper to the Journal of Accountancy and Finance (JAF)?

Submit the full paper (maximum 30 pages, B5 size, MS Word format) to jaf@wyb.ac.lk. Papers must contain Title, Abstract, Keywords, Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results and Discussion, Conclusion and References. The acceptable similarity index is up to 20 percent.

### Q8. Who can I contact for programme applications?

General enquiries: Faculty office — Tel +94 37 228 4215, Email bsfoffice@wyb.ac.lk.
