About the Faculty

The Faculty of Business Studies and Finance (FBSF) — the fastest growing faculty of Wayamba University of Sri Lanka — brings together four academic departments to deliver degree, postgraduate, diploma and external programmes.

Established 1999

History & Establishment

The Faculty of Business Studies and Finance (FBSF) was established on 01 October 1999 by the Government Notification in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 1093/8 of Tuesday, 17 August 1999.

Since its establishment, the faculty has remained the fastest growing faculty of the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka. The faculty is located at the Kuliyapitiya premises of the Wayamba University and consists of four departments of studies, namely, the Department of Accountancy, Department of Banking and Finance, Department of Business Management and Department of Insurance and Valuation.

The departments of Accountancy and Banking & Finance began their pioneering academic roles with the establishment of the University in 2001, being among the earliest academic disciplines introduced.

  • 17 August 1999 Government Notification published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 1093/8
  • 01 October 1999 Faculty of Business Studies and Finance established — the fastest growing faculty of Wayamba University of Sri Lanka
  • 2001 Pioneering academic departments commenced at the University — Departments of Accountancy and Banking & Finance among the earliest disciplines introduced
  • 2014 Accountancy Journal renamed the Journal of Accountancy and Finance and recognized as a refereed journal
  • 2021 Faculty journals commenced — SLJBSF planned its first issue (March 2021); JIF published with effect from January 2021

Institutional Information

Academic & Administrative Structure

Faculty Quality Assurance Cell (FQAC)

The FQAC coordinates quality assurance activities for the improvement of the faculty’s overall performance in liaison with the Centre for Quality Assurance (CQA) of the University. It is chaired by the Dean and coordinates the Quality Assurance Units of the four academic departments, facilitates programme reviews, and plans, implements and evaluates quality assurance activities in liaison with the faculty’s committees.

Learn more

Faculty Committees

FQAC liaises with related committees within the Faculty, including the Curriculum and Academic Development Committee, the Research and Higher Degree Committee, the Teaching and Learning Committee and the Student-Staff Liaison Committee.
