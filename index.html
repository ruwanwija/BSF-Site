About the Faculty

The Faculty of Business Studies and Finance (FBSF) was established on 01 October 1999 by the Government Notification in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 1093/8 of Tuesday, 17 August 1999 and remains the fastest growing faculty of the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka.

The faculty is located at the Kuliyapitiya premises of the Wayamba University and consists of four departments of studies, namely, the Department of Accountancy, Department of Banking and Finance, Department of Business Management and Department of Insurance and Valuation.

From the Office of the Dean

Dean’s Message

You are welcome to the faculty of Business Studies and Finance of the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka. As a fast-growing faculty, we produce quality employable graduates by offering quality Degree programmes with Postgraduate and Diploma programmes. Today, the business sector is flourishing in our country and requires more employees and professionals to meet the demand. Having identified expected qualities and competencies required for the business sector with a keen focusing on nature and trends in that sector FBSF designed its internal and external programmes to cope up with the challenge. You are invited to experience the difference at the faculty by joining our programmes.

Professor SK GamageDean, Faculty of Business Studies and Finance

Pioneers of business education

Department of Business Management

Diploma to masters pathway

Delivers the Postgraduate Diploma in Business Management (PGDBM) leading to the Masters Degree in Business Administration (MBA) — a structured pathway of taught modules, a project and a research thesis.

Department of Insurance & Valuation
