Research Culture

A Faculty Committed to Research Excellence

FBSF’s research ecosystem spans departmental journals, a faculty journal, a student research journal and a professional magazine — all double-blind peer reviewed where applicable. Undergraduates publish in JARSYM and SACFIRE; academics and practitioners publish in JAF, SLJBF, JIF and SLJBSF; and the insurance industry is served by the Voice of Insurance magazine.

Three annual symposia — ARSYM (management research), SACFIRE (accounting & finance research) and SyBFin (banking & finance research) — provide platforms for undergraduates and academics to exchange ideas, present findings and publish proceedings.
