Department of Business Management

Management education from diploma to masters — the PGDBM-to-MBA pathway combining taught modules, a project report and a comprehensive research thesis.

Welcome

Discover the Department of Business Management

The Department of Business Management offers the Postgraduate Diploma in Business Management (PGDBM) leading to the Masters Degree in Business Administration (MBA).

The PGDBM is a programme of study which runs through one academic year (approximately one calendar year duration). Participants follow twelve course modules during the period, of which all course modules are compulsory. Each course module is of about 30 hours of teaching. The academic year comprises two terms; twelve courses are taught during this period. In addition, every participant should complete a project report and submit the same at the end of the 2nd term of the Programme.

The MBA is the second academic year of the total programme. Participants devote their full time to complete management research under the guidance of supervisors appointed by the University, and may attend a series of advanced seminars and lectures organized by the Faculty of Business Studies & Finance.

At a Glance

  • PGDBM leading to MBA programme pathway
  • PGDBM: one academic year, two terms
  • Twelve compulsory course modules (~30 hours each)
  • PGDBM credits: 24 (including project report)
  • MBA: two taught courses (8 credits) + research thesis (8 credits)
  • Direct-entrance route available for the MBA
